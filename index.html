HOW TO PLAY

🔵

Check Stages

Use the LIGHT button to illuminate each animatronic's stage and confirm they are present.

⚡

Controlled Shock

If an animatronic is off-stage, press SHOCK to motivate them back into position.

🎛️

Repair Systems

In the Breaker Room, click the breaker switches in the correct order to restore power.

🔇

Stay Silent

While crawling through Ballora Gallery, move slowly. Ballora tracks sound — stay quiet!

🛡️

Defend Yourself

In Parts/Service, watch Funtime Freddy's danger meter. Play Bon-Bon's audio to calm him.

🔋

Manage Power

In the Private Room, keep the power generator running to survive until 6 AM.
